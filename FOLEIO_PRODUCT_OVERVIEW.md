## Short answers to your questions

### 1. Is the website just for my customers to place bookings, and you take a percentage?

**Yes — that’s the core idea.**

Foleio gives you a **public page** (your own link) where **your customers** can:

- Book your services  
- Buy from your shop (if you use shop)  
- Pay online  

When a customer pays, **Foleio takes a platform & service fee** from that payment. The rest settles to **your linked Nigerian bank account** (via Paystack) — typically the next business day. There is no separate “withdraw” step.

You are **not** charged a booking fee on top by us beyond the plan fee below. Customers pay the price **you** set.

### 2. Does the website bring customers for people who signed up?

**Not automatically today.**

Foleio is primarily a **storefront + bookings + payments** tool. You share your link (Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, etc.), and customers come through **your** audience.

We do **not** currently run a marketplace that sends you a stream of new customers just because you signed up.

**Coming soon**

- **SEO** — so your public page can rank better in Google over time  
- **Ads** — optional paid promotion to help bring in new customers  

Until those launch, growth is driven by **you sharing your page** and serving clients well.

## Fees & plans (current)

Two plans. Same idea: Free costs nothing monthly; Pro costs a subscription and takes a **lower** fee per sale.

### Free — ₦0 / month

- Full access to bookings, shop, and tools  
- **3.5% + ₦100** platform & service fee per transaction  
- Up to 10 services and 10 products (5 preorders)  
- One Home portfolio gallery  

### Pro — ₦3,000 / month or ₦7,500 / quarter (save ~17%)

- Everything on Free  
- **1.8% + ₦100** platform & service fee per transaction  
- Unlimited services & products  
- Up to 3 portfolio categories  
- Schedule templates  
- Self-serve upgrade anytime in **Settings → Billing**  

**Example fees**

| Sale amount | Free (3.5% + ₦100) | Pro (1.8% + ₦100) | You save with Pro |
|-------------|--------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| ₦5,000 | ₦275 | ₦190 | ₦85 |
| ₦10,000 | ₦450 | ₦280 | ₦170 |
| ₦200,000 | ₦7,100 | ₦3,700 | ₦3,400 |
| ₦300,000 | ₦10,600 | ₦5,500 | ₦5,100 |

Rough guide: Pro often “pays for itself” once you’re doing about **₦176k+/month** in sales (monthly plan) or about **₦147k+/month** (quarterly plan).

## How a booking payment works

1. Customer opens **your** Foleio link  
2. They pick a service / date and pay  
3. Fee is taken from the payment (Free or Pro rate)  
4. Your share settles to your linked bank on Paystack’s schedule  

You keep control of **prices**, **availability**, and **cancellation policy**.
