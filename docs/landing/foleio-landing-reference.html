Questions, answered.

How do I get paid?+

Your share of each payment settles directly to the Nigerian bank account you link, typically the next business day. Weekends and bank holidays can add time.

Can I take a deposit instead of full payment?+

Yes. You choose a deposit or full payment for each service. The balance is due before the booking date, seven days by default, and your client gets a reminder.

What happens if a client cancels?+

You set your own cancellation and refund policy. Clients see it before they pay.

Do my clients need a Foleio account?+

No. They open your link, choose what they want, and pay.

What do I need to get started?+

You need to be 18 or older and have a Nigerian bank account in your own name.

Who handles the payments?+

Paystack processes every payment. Clients can pay by card, bank, and other Paystack channels. Foleio never stores card details.

Is Foleio available outside Nigeria?+

Not yet. Foleio is currently for creators based in Nigeria.

Can I cancel Pro?+

Any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, then move back to Free.
