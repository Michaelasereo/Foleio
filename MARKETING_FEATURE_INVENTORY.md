# Foleio Feature Inventory

Marketing-ready overview for competitive analysis, positioning, sales enablement, and roadmap comparison.

## 1. Product Summary

Foleio is a creator business platform for turning a creator profile into a monetized storefront, booking page, content hub, portfolio, and fan/customer management system.

The platform combines:

- Public creator profiles
- Booking and appointment workflows
- Digital content and collections
- Shop/product selling
- Creator earnings and payouts
- Fan/customer dashboards
- Admin, moderation, reconciliation, and platform operations
- Payments through Paystack
- Media storage and delivery through Cloudflare services

Core positioning:

> Foleio helps creators run the business around their work: bookings, products, paid content, payouts, public profiles, and customer relationships in one place.

## 2. Primary User Groups

### Creators

Creators use Foleio to publish their work, sell services, sell products, manage bookings, collect payments, track earnings, and manage their public business presence.

Relevant creator types include:

- Creative professionals
- Service-based creators
- Coaches and consultants
- Digital product sellers
- Educators and tutorial creators
- Portfolio-based freelancers
- Influencers monetizing audiences
- Small creator-led businesses

### Fans And Customers

Fans and customers use Foleio to discover creators, book services, buy products, access paid content, track orders, manage subscriptions, and follow booking/payment status.

### Platform Admins

Admins use Foleio to manage creators, payouts, waitlists, moderation, reconciliation, billing, transactions, platform settings, and operational integrity.
